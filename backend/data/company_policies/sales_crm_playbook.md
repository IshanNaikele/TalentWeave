# Sales CRM Playbook — TalentWeave Internal

## Target Role: Sales Team

## 1. CRM Setup (Salesforce)
- Login to Salesforce at talentweave.salesforce.com
- Request your CRM credentials from the Operations team on Day 1
- Complete the mandatory Salesforce onboarding module within Day 3

## 2. Lead Management
- All inbound leads are auto-imported from the website contact form
- Assign leads to yourself within 2 hours of notification
- Update lead status after every interaction (Contacted, Qualified, Proposal)

## 3. Cold Calling Scripts
- Always introduce yourself and the company within the first 10 seconds
- Key opening line: "Hi, I'm calling from TalentWeave — we help companies automate their hiring pipeline using AI."
- Listen for pain points around manual hiring, slow onboarding, or high recruiter costs

## 4. Product Pricing Tiers
- Starter Plan: $299/month — up to 50 job postings
- Growth Plan: $799/month — unlimited postings + AI screening
- Enterprise Plan: Custom pricing — full white-label + dedicated support

## 5. Outbound Email Sequences
- Day 1: Introduction email with one-liner value proposition
- Day 3: Follow-up with a relevant case study attachment
- Day 7: Final nudge with a calendar booking link
- Use the approved templates from the shared Google Drive folder

## 6. Monthly Targets
- Inbound Sales Exec: 20 qualified demos per month
- Outbound Account Executive: 15 closed deals per quarter
- Enterprise Sales Lead: 3 enterprise contracts per quarter
- Track all metrics inside the Salesforce dashboard weekly
